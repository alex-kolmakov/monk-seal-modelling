Notes and sources

Part of the Projeto de Vigilância do Estado de Conservação do Lobo-marinho no arquipélago da Madeira (lobomarinhomadeira.com), with the support of the Monk Seal Alliance and the Instituto das Florestas e Conservação da Natureza.

Waves (VHM0), swell (VHM0_SW1) and tide (sea surface height) are Copernicus Marine IBI forecast values at the sea pixel nearest each cave. Wave direction is the mean direction the waves come from. Thresholds marked "call" come from the programme coordinator at IFCN (22 Oct 2026), "assumed" are our own placeholders. Cave positions are placed by rule on the coast (Tabaqueiro south-west, Bufador east), not surveyed. The 3 km wave grid cannot show swell funnelling into a cave mouth, so a green window is optimistic.
